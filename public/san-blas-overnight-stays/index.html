Sleep steps from the Caribbean surf in traditional beachfront and overwater cabanas. Untamed Caribbean stars, digital detox, and authentic Guna hospitality.

Experience Highlights

Beachfront & Overwater Cabanas

Rustic island dwellings built with local hardwood and thatched palm roofs, positioned directly on the sand or standing over the shallow lagoon.

Private & Shared Bathroom Options

Choose comfortable cabins with ensuite private bathrooms (such as on Isla Chichime Grande) or authentic traditional shared facility stays.

All Daily Meals Included

Full island board featuring fresh seafood, coconut rice, fried plantains, and tropical fruit prepared by local Guna families.

Daily Boat Excursions to Outer Cays

Every full day of your stay includes guided boat trips to neighboring uninhabited islands, barrier reef drop-offs, and starfish sandbanks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the cabins like and what amenities are provided?

Cabins are authentic eco-rustic structures built from local wood and thatched palm. Solar or generator electricity operates during evening hours.

Is there fresh water and how do the bathrooms work?

Host islands have freshwater reserves for rinsing and showering. Select islands offer private ensuite bathrooms.

Is there Wi-Fi or reliable phone coverage?

Cellular reception is faint and outer islands have no Wi-Fi, offering guests a true digital detox.
