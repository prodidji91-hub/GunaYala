Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a catamaran and a monohull sailboat?

Catamarans offer dual-hull stability, minimal heel, and expansive trampolines. Monohulls offer a romantic classic sailing experience.

Is sailing in San Blas safe from storms?

San Blas lies completely outside the Caribbean hurricane belt and is protected by extensive coral reef systems.

Can we customize our sailing route and island stops?

Yes, charters are 100% private. Petra and your captain will customize your itinerary based on weather, wind, and preferences.
