San Blas Day Trips from Panama City

Full-day Caribbean island-hopping excursion into the pristine Guna Yala archipelago. Crystal turquoise waters, starfish sandbanks, and fresh traditional catch.

Experience Highlights

2 to 3 Pristine Coral Islands

Explore postcard-perfect cayes including Isla Perro Chico, famous for its offshore shipwreck reef bustling with marine life.

Natural Pool Sandbank Snorkel

Wade waist-deep in open ocean on a shallow sandbank surrounded by starfish and turquoise transparency.

Traditional Fresh Catch Lunch

Enjoy authentic whole fried Caribbean catch or fresh chicken with coconut rice and salad, prepared by our Guna hosts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a day trip from Panama City enough time to enjoy San Blas?

Yes, departing at 5:00 AM allows over 6 hours of island relaxation, swimming, and reef snorkeling before afternoon return.

What travel documents are required at the checkpoint?

An original physical passport with at least 6 months validity (or Panamanian cédula) is strictly required by Comarca border authorities.

What happens in case of rough weather or marine advisories?

We operate in strict coordination with SINAPROC and maritime port authorities. Port closures offer free rescheduling or full refunds.
